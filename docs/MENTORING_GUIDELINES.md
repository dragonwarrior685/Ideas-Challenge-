# Mentoring Guidelines - Moana Pathways

## For Mentors

### Code of Conduct

**Commitment to Excellence:**
- Respond to messages within 48 hours
- Be authentic and share real experiences
- Maintain confidentiality of mentee information
- Celebrate small wins and progress
- Model professional behavior and integrity

**Boundaries:**
- Clearly communicate availability
- Don't provide medical/legal/financial advice
- Redirect inappropriate conversations
- Maintain professional tone
- Respect mentee autonomy in decision-making

### Mentoring Best Practices

1. **First Connection**
   - Welcome mentee warmly
   - Share your story briefly
   - Ask about their interests and goals
   - Set clear expectations for mentoring

2. **Active Listening**
   - Ask clarifying questions
   - Don't jump to solutions
   - Validate their experiences
   - Acknowledge challenges they face

3. **Advice Giving**
   - Share relevant experiences
   - Offer multiple perspectives
   - Empower them to decide
   - Follow up on their progress

4. **Cultural Humility**
   - Learn from mentee's perspective
   - Acknowledge power dynamics
   - Respect cultural protocols
   - Center mentee's voice

### Sample Responses

**When asked about career choice:**
> "I chose marine biology because I wanted to protect our oceans. It wasn't a straight path—I studied chemistry first! What aspects of environmental work interest you most?"

**When mentee faces self-doubt:**
> "That feeling is so normal. I doubted myself constantly. But look at what you've already accomplished... Let's break down the challenge into smaller steps."

**When mentee asks for advice:**
> "Here's what worked for me, but your path might look different. What do you think? What feels right to you?"

## For Players

### Getting the Most from Mentorship

1. **Be Respectful**
   - Respect mentor's time
   - Be punctual for scheduled calls
   - Thank mentors for their time
   - Ask permission before sharing advice publicly

2. **Come Prepared**
   - Ask thoughtful questions
   - Share context about your situation
   - Follow up on advice they give
   - Update them on progress

3. **Engage Authentically**
   - Be honest about challenges
   - Share your real interests
   - Don't pretend to know things you don't
   - Ask for clarification

4. **Remember**
   - Mentors are volunteers
   - They may not have all answers
   - Patience with response times
   - Everyone started where you are

### Questions to Ask Your Mentor

- "How did you get started in this field?"
- "What was your biggest challenge along the way?"
- "How do you stay motivated?"
- "What skills do I need to develop?"
- "Can you recommend resources for learning more?"
- "How do you balance work and personal life?"
- "What advice would you give your younger self?"
- "How do you deal with imposter syndrome?"
