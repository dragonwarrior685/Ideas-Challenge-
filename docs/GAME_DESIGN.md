# Moana Pathways - Game Design Document

## 1. Overview

Moana Pathways is a culturally-responsive career-aspirational game designed for Māori and Pacific girls aged 10-18. It combines engaging Subway Surfers-style gameplay with real-world mentoring connections and skill-building challenges.

## 2. Core Loop

**5-Minute Session:**
1. Player enters Runner Mode
2. Navigate obstacles, collect skill tokens
3. Complete a mini-challenge (Wordle-style)
4. Unlock character progress
5. Check mentor notifications/messages

## 3. Progression System

- **Score-based**: Points from obstacles and challenges
- **Character-based**: Unlock characters by hitting milestones
- **Skill-based**: Accumulate skills through challenges
- **Relationship-based**: Mentor connections deepen over time

## 4. Win Conditions

- Reach career goal (personality test result)
- Unlock all 9 characters
- Complete mentor connections across 5+ fields
- Build exportable CV
- Share achievements with community

## 5. Player Demographics

- Primary: Girls 10-18, Māori & Pacific heritage
- Secondary: Teachers, Career counselors, Parents
- Tertiary: Mentors from target communities

## 6. Key Success Factors

- ✅ Fast, addictive runner mechanics
- ✅ Representation and cultural authenticity
- ✅ Real mentor impact and responsiveness
- ✅ Tangible skill development tracking
- ✅ Career clarity through play
