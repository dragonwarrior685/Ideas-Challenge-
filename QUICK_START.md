# 🎮 How to Play Moana Pathways - Quick Start Guide\n\n## Option 1: Play Online (Easiest - No Installation!) ✅\n\n### Coming Soon! 🚀\nWe're deploying the game to be playable directly in your browser.\n\n**For now, use Option 2 below to play locally.**\n\n---\n\n## Option 2: Play on Your Computer (Takes 5 Minutes)\n\n### **Step 1: Install Node.js**\n1. Go to https://nodejs.org/\n2. Download the **LTS (Long Term Support)** version\n3. Install it (click next through all screens)\n4. Open your terminal/command prompt and type:\n   ```\n   node --version\n   ```\n   You should see a version number like `v18.0.0` ✅\n\n### **Step 2: Get the Game Code**\n\n**Option A - Using Git (Recommended)**\n```bash\ngit clone https://github.com/dragonwarrior685/Ideas-Challenge-.git\ncd Ideas-Challenge-\n```\n\n**Option B - Download as ZIP**\n1. Go to: https://github.com/dragonwarrior685/Ideas-Challenge-\n2. Click **Code** (green button) → **Download ZIP**\n3. Unzip the folder\n4. Open terminal in that folder\n\n### **Step 3: Install & Run**\n\n```bash\n# Install game dependencies\nnpm install\n\n# Start the game\nnpm run dev\n```\n\n**Wait 10-15 seconds...** ⏳\n\nYour browser will automatically open to:\n```\nhttp://localhost:8080\n```\n\n✅ **GAME IS RUNNING!** 🎮\n\n---\n\n## Playing the Game 🕹️\n\n### **Main Menu** (First Screen)\nYou'll see 3 big buttons:\n\n#### 1. **PLAY GAME** 🎯\n- **Controls:**\n  - **Arrow Keys Left/Right** = Move left & right\n  - **SPACE Bar or Arrow Up** = Jump\n  - **RED blocks** = Avoid them! Game Over if you hit one\n  - **GOLD squares** = Collect for +50 points\n\n- **Goal:** Stay alive as long as possible, collect tokens\n- **Levels:** Get harder as you score more points\n- **Score:** \n  - +10 pts per obstacle you pass\n  - +50 pts per gold token collected\n\n#### 2. **FIND MENTOR** 🤝\n- **Browse 3 mentors:**\n  1. **Kahu** - Environmental Guardian (Marine Conservation)\n  2. **Aroha** - Designer & Artist (Creative Expression)\n  3. **Malia** - Tech Founder (Startups & Coding)\n\n- **How to connect:**\n  - Click on any mentor card\n  - View their profile\n  - Click **CONNECT** to send a message\n  - ✅ You'll get confirmation!\n\n#### 3. **CAREER QUIZ** 🧠\n- **Answer 3 quick questions** about yourself\n- **Get your career match:**\n  - 🎨 Artist & Designer\n  - 💻 Tech Innovator\n  - 🏥 Healthcare Hero\n  - 💼 Business Leader\n  - 🌍 Environmental Champion\n\n- **See recommended mentors** for your career path\n- **Go to FIND MENTOR** to connect with them!\n\n---\n\n## 🎮 Game Controls Quick Reference\n\n| Action | Key |\n|--------|-----|\n| Move Left | **←** (Left Arrow) |\n| Move Right | **→** (Right Arrow) |\n| Jump | **SPACE** or **↑** (Up Arrow) |\n| Go Back to Menu | Click **MENU** button |\n| Restart Game | Click **RESTART** button |\n\n---\n\n## 🚀 Troubleshooting\n\n### **\"Command not found: npm\"**\n→ Node.js didn't install. Go back to Step 1 and restart your computer after installing.\n\n### **\"Port 8080 already in use\"**\n→ Another program is using that port. Try:\n```bash\nnpm run dev -- --port 8081\n```\n\n### **Game won't load**\n→ Wait 15 seconds for webpack to compile. Check the terminal for errors.\n\n### **Graphics look weird**\n→ Refresh your browser (press F5 or Cmd+R)\n\n---\n\n## 🎯 Tips for Success\n\n✨ **Runner Game Tips:**\n- Plan your moves - obstacles come faster at higher levels\n- Gold tokens = 5x more points than passing obstacles\n- Don't panic on Level 5+ - slower, calculated movements win\n- Your high score is saved (try to beat it!)\n\n✨ **Mentor Tips:**\n- Connect with mentors in fields you're interested in\n- Each mentor has different expertise\n- Check back to see responses (coming soon!)\n\n✨ **Career Quiz Tips:**\n- Answer honestly - your matches are based on your answers\n- You can retake the quiz anytime\n- Try different answers to see other career paths\n\n---\n\n## 🌟 Coming Soon!\n\n- 📞 Real mentor responses & messaging\n- 📊 CV builder to export your skills\n- 🏆 Achievement badges & leaderboards\n- 🎨 Bro Town-style characters\n- 🔊 Pacific Island soundtracks\n- 🎮 More challenging puzzles\n- 💼 Skill challenges (Wordle-style)\n\n---\n\n## ❓ Questions?\n\n**If something doesn't work:**\n1.
