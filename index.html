📖 HOW TO PLAY

OBJECTIVE:

Guide Moana through obstacles and meet mentors on her career path!

GAMEPLAY:

• Click on Moana to move her forward

• Avoid obstacles by answering career questions

• Meet mentors to learn about real careers

• Answer questions correctly to earn points!

SCORING:

• +10 points: Meet a mentor

• +5 points: Answer correctly

• -5 points: Answer incorrectly
