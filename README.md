# Moana Pathways 🌊
## A Career-Aspirational Runner Game for Māori & Pacific Girls

### Vision
A vibrant, culturally-centered game that combines:
- **Subway Surfers-style gameplay** with Bro Town aesthetic
- **Playable leaders & role models** from diverse Māori/Pacific backgrounds
- **Skill-building challenges** (Wordle-style puzzles)
- **Career pathway discovery** through personality-driven scenarios
- **CV building system** that tracks achievements and skills
- **Mentor networking system** connecting players to real role models & community leaders

---

## 🎮 Core Gameplay Mechanics

### 1. Runner Mode (Main Loop)
- Navigate obstacles while collecting **Skill Tokens** 🔷
- Unlock **Leader Characters** through milestones
- Earn **Challenge Cards** that open story scenarios
- Collect **Career Clues** that hint at role model professions
- Build **Mentor Connections** as you progress

### 2. Challenge Scenarios (Wordle-Style)
Players solve contextual puzzles based on their character's domain:
- **Business Leader**: Market trends puzzle
- **Environmental Scientist**: Climate pattern matching
- **Doctor/Health Worker**: Diagnosis challenge
- **Engineer**: System debugging puzzle
- **Artist/Creator**: Design pattern completion

Solving unlocks:
- Skill badges (Communication, Problem-Solving, Leadership, etc.)
- CV achievements
- Story progression
- Character backstories
- **Mentor introductions & networking tokens**

### 3. Personality Quiz → Career Paths
Quick 5-question personality test reveals:
- Primary career interest
- Secondary opportunities
- Recommended characters to unlock
- Matching challenge types
- **Suggested mentors to connect with**

### 4. CV Building System
Players accumulate:
- **Skills** from challenges completed
- **Achievements** from runner milestones
- **Character mentor profiles** (which leaders they've unlocked)
- **Experience log** exportable as visual CV
- **Mentor connection history** (testimonials, advice received)

### 5. Mentor Networking System (NEW) 🤝
**In-Game Mentor Directory:**
- Browse **verified mentors** across different career paths
- Filter by: profession, ethnicity, mentoring style, availability
- View mentor profiles with:
  - Professional background & achievements
  - Advice clips (video/audio snippets)
  - Skills they can help develop
  - Availability for chats/calls
  - Success stories of mentees

**Mentor Connection Flow:**
1. Unlock mentor availability through gameplay
2. Send **Connection Requests** (free, with personalized message)
3. **Mentor Accepts/Responds** via in-app messaging
4. Schedule **Micro-Mentoring Sessions**:
   - Quick 15-min chat prompts
   - Monthly video calls
   - Email Q&A exchanges
5. Receive **Mentor Badges** unlocking new challenges
6. Share mentor advice in player community

## 🌟 Characters (Unlockable Leaders)

### Tier 1 (Early Game)
- **Aroha (Artist/Designer)** - Bro Town-inspired illustrator
  - Mentor: Available for design/creative mentoring
- **Kahu (Environmental Guardian)** - Marine conservation leader
  - Mentor: Expertise in sustainability & advocacy
- **Malia (Tech Founder)** - Startup entrepreneur
  - Mentor: Business strategy & tech pathways

### Tier 2 (Mid Game)
- **Rangi (Sports Coach/Athlete)** - Leadership through mentorship
  - Mentor: Performance, resilience, team dynamics
- **Hera (Healthcare Worker)** - Nurse/midwife pathway
  - Mentor: Healthcare careers & community care
- **Tai (Engineer)** - Infrastructure & innovation
  - Mentor: Problem-solving, STEM, project management

### Tier 3 (Late Game)
- **Moana (Explorer/Innovator)** - Cross-discipline visionary
  - Mentor: Visionary thinking, career pivots
- **Lani (Community Leader)** - Social change advocate
  - Mentor: Activism, community organizing, impact
- **Pata (Cultural Curator)** - Arts & heritage champion
  - Mentor: Cultural preservation, storytelling, identity
